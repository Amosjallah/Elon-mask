Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Elon Space & Innovation Network respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when interacting with our website and network.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you submit a Network Inquiry or interact with our website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, country, date of birth, inquiry details, and related information.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Information may be used to:

  • Review and respond to your inquiry
  • Communicate with you regarding your request
  • Provide relevant information about opportunities or services
  • Improve our website and network
  • Meet applicable administrative, security, and legal needs

3. INFORMATION PROTECTION

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our website. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. INFORMATION SHARING

We do not sell personal information submitted through the website. Information may be shared with authorized personnel or service providers when reasonably necessary to process requests, operate the website, or comply with applicable law.

5. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

You may contact us regarding questions about information you have submitted and, where applicable, request access, correction or deletion of your information.

6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, forms, analytics, communications, payment processing, or other website functions. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. POLICY UPDATES

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

8. CONTACT US

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Elon Space & Innovation Network through our Network Inquiry page.
